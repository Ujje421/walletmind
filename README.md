# 🧠 WalletMind

> **Your AI-Powered Personal Finance Assistant**

Track your income and expenses as naturally as sending a message.

WalletMind transforms personal finance into a conversation using AI, natural language, voice, and intelligent automation.

---

## ✨ Why WalletMind?

Traditional finance apps require users to:

❌ Open the app  
❌ Tap "Add Transaction"  
❌ Select Income or Expense  
❌ Choose a Category  
❌ Enter an Amount  
❌ Save

WalletMind removes this friction.

Simply type:

```text
Coffee 180
Salary 65000
Paid Rent 15000
Netflix 649
Bought Groceries 2400
```

WalletMind automatically understands:

- 💰 Income or Expense
- 💵 Amount
- 📂 Category
- 🏪 Merchant
- 📅 Date
- ⏰ Time
- 💳 Payment Method
- 📝 Notes

No forms.
No manual categorization.
Just conversations.

# 🚀 Features

### 🤖 AI Transaction Recognition

Record transactions using natural language.

Examples:

```text
Coffee 180

Lunch with Rahul 650

Salary credited 65000

Paid electricity bill 2400

Petrol yesterday 1200

Dad sent me 5000
```

---

### 💬 Chat-First Experience

Forget forms.

Talk to your finances like ChatGPT.

Examples:

```text
How much did I spend on food this month?

Show all UPI payments.

How much did I save last year?

Show expenses above ₹5000.

What are my subscriptions?
```

WalletMind converts speech into structured financial records.
